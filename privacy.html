Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

ViralBoost Pro respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

2. Information We Access

When you choose to connect your Google and YouTube account, the application may access your YouTube channel information and video upload permissions through the YouTube Data API.

Access is provided only after you grant permission through Google's authorization process.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through YouTube is used to display channel information and perform video upload actions requested by the user.

Firebase Authentication may be used to authenticate application users. Firebase Firestore may store limited account and application information.

4. Data Storage and Security

YouTube access tokens are used by the application to communicate with Google's services. The application does not intentionally store Google account passwords.

Some application history and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

5. Third-Party Services

The application uses Google services, including Google OAuth, YouTube Data API and Firebase. Their use is also subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. User Choices

You can disconnect your YouTube account and revoke application access through your Google Account security settings.

You can stop using the application at any time.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application developer using the developer contact email listed on the Google Auth Platform branding page.
